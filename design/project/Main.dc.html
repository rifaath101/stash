Product brief · v1

Stash

Save any article or YouTube video in one paste. Stash reads it, writes a short summary and tags, and lets you find it later by describing what it was about.

The problem

Good links get lost in bookmarks, open tabs and chats with yourself. Weeks later you remember what an article was about, but not its title or where you put it.

Who it is for

People who learn online every day: developers, students, anyone who reads docs and watches tutorials. First user: you.

What success looks like

You type a vague memory of a link, and the right one is in the top three results. You actually use it while building it.

How it works

1
Save
Paste a link to an article or a YouTube video. It appears in your library at once, marked "Reading…".
2
Stash reads it
In the background it pulls the page text (or the video's transcript), then AI writes a summary, key points and tags.
3
Find it by meaning
Search the way you remember it, like "why is my query slow". Results match the idea, not just the words.

In v1

  • Save a YouTube link (uses its captions)
  • AI summary, key points and tags
  • Library with filters
  • Search by meaning
  • Edit tags, delete a link
